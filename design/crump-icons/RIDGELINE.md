# Crump waveform relief icons

The requested direction combines a synth wavetable with the stacked-signal aesthetic of Unknown Pleasures: multiple waveform traces collectively form a C. These are original C-shaped compositions, using `05-wavetable/source.png` as the palette and tile reference.

Open [ridgeline.html](ridgeline.html) to compare the two marks and their scaled favicon previews.

- **Ridgeline C** (`07-ridgeline-c/`): Lime waveform ridges build a C, with a clear open center and a sculpted wavetable feel.
- **Signal Relief** (`08-signal-relief/`): A denser ivory signal stack, with peaks shaping a C in the spirit of a vintage scientific plot.

Each folder contains `source.png` (original generated artwork), `icon.png` (512px), `favicon-16.png`, `favicon-32.png`, `favicon-48.png`, and `favicon.ico` containing 16/32/48px frames. The built-in imagegen tool created the artwork; ImageMagick performed only size and format exports. Ridgeline C uses a separately generated six-wave companion for its 16/32/48px favicon exports. The detailed large mark stays in source.png and icon.png; the compact drawing is preserved in compact-source.png and compact.png. Signal Relief uses direct reductions of its dense mark and is best viewed at larger sizes.
