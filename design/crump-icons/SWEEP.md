# Crump oscillator sweep variations

Refinements of Signal Relief using a deliberate visual progression from sine waves at the top, through triangle waves, to square/pulse waves at the bottom. The aim is a synth-specific identity with less of the earlier irregular ridge-plot character. This progression is a design choice for these icons.

Open [sweep.html](sweep.html) to compare colors, waveform irregularity, and actual-size favicon previews.

- **Glacier** (`09-glacier-sweep/`): Clean periodic waves: cyan sine curves morph through triangles into blue square waves. No added noise.
- **Ember** (`10-ember-drift/`): Apricot and coral traces with slight phase drift. Smooth sine at the top, warm square-wave steps below.
- **Acid** (`11-acid-quantize/`): Citron and mint with controlled digital stepping. A rougher oscillator sweep with square waves anchoring the base.

Each folder contains `source.png` (original generated artwork), `icon.png` (512px), `favicon-16.png`, `favicon-32.png`, `favicon-48.png`, and `favicon.ico` containing 16/32/48px frames. The favicons are direct size reductions of the detailed concepts. The built-in imagegen tool generated these variants with `08-signal-relief/source.png` as the composition reference. ImageMagick performed only size and format exports.
