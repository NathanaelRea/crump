# Crump synth icon options

Refinement of the lime Step Sequencer direction into waveform and wavetable symbols. Open [synth.html](synth.html) for the three options, actual-size previews on light and dark backgrounds, and downloadable PNG/ICO files.

- **Square Wave** (`04-square-wave/`): A bold square-wave cycle. The most direct oscillator symbol, with a crisp terminal feel.
- **Wavetable** (`05-wavetable/`): Three stacked waveforms suggest a wavetable scan. The strongest connection to synthesis.
- **Wave C** (`06-wave-c/`): A C-shaped oscillator loop with a wave entering its center. A more distinctive Crump monogram.

Each folder contains the final generated `source.png`, a 512px `icon.png`, `favicon-16.png`, `favicon-32.png`, `favicon-48.png`, and a multi-frame `favicon.ico` with those three sizes. The built-in imagegen tool generated these variants using `02-step-sequencer/source.png` as the style reference. ImageMagick is used only for size and format exports. The original options remain available.
