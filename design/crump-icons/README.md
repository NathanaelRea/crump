## Generation prompts

### Pulse C

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: app icon and browser favicon for Crump, a terminal-first electronic music production instrument. Create ONE finished icon, square canvas, centered. Bold flat vector-like design with precise edges, a memorable silhouette, and very few geometric parts that remain clear at 16x16 pixels. A nearly black charcoal (#131619) rounded square tile occupies 94% of the canvas, with transparent pixels outside the tile; the symbol fills about 70% of the tile. No lettering other than an implied abstract C, no wordmark, no caption, no mockup, no shadows, no lighting effects, no gradients, no texture, no 3D, no tiny details, no thin lines. Primary request: a distinctive warm orange (#FF784F) geometric C monogram whose silhouette suggests a square audio pulse. Build one heavy continuous rectilinear C, with softened outer corners, a wide open mouth to the right and a short offset terminal at the upper right suggesting the start of a pulse. The C itself must be immediately readable, confident, tightly composed. Broad negative space, balanced geometric proportions. The entire orange mark is a single solid shape, no separate ornaments.

No lettering other than an implied abstract C, no wordmark, no caption, no mockup, no shadows, no lighting effects, no gradients, no texture, no 3D, no tiny details, no thin lines. Primary request: an acid lime (#C6F36B) abstract step-sequencer C. Form a compact open C using exactly five generously sized square pads: three stacked vertically on the left, one upper-right pad, one lower-right pad. Equal narrow gaps, slightly rounded pad corners. Together these five pads make a 2-column, 3-row grid with the middle-right pad absent, giving a clear chunky open C. The two right pads can be offset slightly farther right for a broad silhouette. It should evoke musical sequencing and terminal blocks. Strict simplicity and bold rhythmic geometry.
