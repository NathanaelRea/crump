## Question

What exact Creative Brief, Sound Brief, 30-60 second Reference Project fixture, Beginner Musician procedure, Experienced Producer procedure, machine checks, measurements, artifacts, and pass/fail record prove the canonical First Public Release without reintroducing complete-track scope?

## Research Baseline

Use the product contract and release interpretation as the complete scope authority. Freeze one reproducible fixture and separate beginner-accessibility, producer-credibility, real-time, persistence, history, Agent, MIDI Note Audition, and render evidence; neither human result substitutes for the other, and no deferred capability may satisfy a release item.
