## Question

What installation, update, exact-build compatibility, Agent harness/model evidence, no-model core, diagnostics, documentation, Reference Project, audio/MIDI hardware, failure-reporting, and known-exclusion commitments must be met before publishing the narrow release?

## Research Baseline

Use Ubuntu/PipeWire packaging and mature open-source support practices as applicable to the accepted slice. State the exact supported environment and transient MIDI fixture, one tested external harness/model configuration without guaranteeing its future price, the complete manual core, and every deferred capability. Do not make plugin, sample, Rust-extension, desktop, or complete-track support decisions.
